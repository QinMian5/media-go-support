Media Go support

Media Go is an iPhone audio capture tool for developers. Configure capture buttons, purposes, and annotation fields, then upload recordings to your own receiver. You can also record, annotate, and play audio locally.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 26 or later. The first App Store release will be free and available in the United States. TestFlight testers each configure their own receiver.

Start recording

  1. Open Capture and tap a capture button. The default button is Record.
  2. Allow microphone access, then tap Stop when finished.
  3. Review playback and annotations, then confirm. With no receiver configured, the recording stays on your device.
  4. Open Settings → Capture buttons to create buttons, set a purpose, add annotation fields, or choose a delivery mode.

Connect your receiver

  1. Open Settings → Receiver and enter the full HTTP or HTTPS endpoint URL. Use HTTPS for a public endpoint.
  2. If your server requires authentication, enable Send Authorization and enter the complete header value, such as Bearer your-token. Credentials are stored in Keychain.
  3. Tap Test connection. A compatible server returns {"status":"ok"}.
  4. Tap Save. Pending items can resume delivery. Items already bound to a receiver continue to use that receiver.

Your server must implement the Media Go receiver API. A generic file-upload URL is not enough. The app does not provide a public media server for everyday use.

Delivery modes

Whole recording sends one complete WAV file after you stop and confirm annotations. Nothing is sent before confirmation.

Continuous upload sends WAV segments while recording and submits the recording manifest and final annotations after you stop and confirm. Segment length is configurable from 1 to 600 seconds.

Playback, cleanup, and deletion

Open Media to review recordings and retry uploads. In Settings → Local audio, choose Never, 1 day, 7 days, or 30 days for cleanup after a completed upload. Cleanup retains records and annotations but removes local playback.

Deleting an item removes its local audio and app record and abandons pending uploads. It does not recall segments already sent or delete content on your receiver. See the privacy policy.

Troubleshooting

  • Microphone unavailable: check iOS Settings and your audio device.
  • Unable to connect: check the URL, network, VPN or Tailscale connection, and TLS certificate.
  • Authentication failed: check the full Authorization value and server configuration.
  • Not a Media Go receiver: verify the multipart API and test response.
  • Storage warning: free space. Unfinished uploads are retained rather than automatically deleted.
